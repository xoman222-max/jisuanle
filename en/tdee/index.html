How it works

Your TDEE is roughly how many calories you burn per day. Eat about 500 kcal less to lose weight steadily, or a little more to support muscle gain. Results are estimates.

FAQ

Which formula is used?

Mifflin-St Jeor, one of the most accurate for most adults.

How fast can I lose weight?

A 500 kcal daily deficit is about 0.5 kg per week.
