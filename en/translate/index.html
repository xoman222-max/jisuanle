How to use

Paste up to 1000 characters, pick the target language and get a fluent translation. Useful for product pages, emails, menus and signs while traveling. To keep it free for everyone there is a daily site-wide limit, shown as “remaining today”. Text is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI for translation and is not stored; avoid entering passwords or sensitive personal data.

FAQ

Is my text stored?

No, it is translated in real time by Cloudflare Workers AI and not saved by this site.

How much can I translate at once?

Up to 1000 characters per request.

Why is the button disabled?

Today’s shared quota may be used up; it resets at midnight (Taipei time).
