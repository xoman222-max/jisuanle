How to use

An open-source U²-Net model runs locally in your browser to separate the subject from the background. The first run downloads the model (~4.6 MB), then it is cached. Works best with clear subjects such as people, products and pets. Everything runs in your browser — your images are never uploaded.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — the AI runs on your device, so there are no credits or limits.

Why are edges rough?

Fine hair and busy backgrounds are hard; use a clearer photo for better results.
