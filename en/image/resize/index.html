How to use

Pick a preset like Instagram 1080×1350 or YouTube thumbnail 1280×720, or enter your own width and height. “Fit” adds a background color, “Fill” crops to fill the frame. Everything runs in your browser — your images are never uploaded.

FAQ

Fit vs. fill?

Fit keeps the whole image and pads it; fill crops edges to cover the frame.

Can I resize many images?

Yes, select multiple files and download them as a ZIP.
