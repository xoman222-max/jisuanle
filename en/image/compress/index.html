How to use

Phone photos are often 3–10 MB — slow to email or upload. Re-encode them here: a JPG/WebP quality of 70–80 usually looks identical yet saves 60–90%, and limiting the longest side to 1920 px is perfect for web and chat. Everything runs in your browser — your images are never uploaded.

FAQ

What quality should I use?

70–80 for photos; 85–92 if you need fine detail or printing.

Why does PNG barely shrink?

PNG is lossless — export photos as JPG or WebP instead.

Is EXIF kept?

No. Re-encoding drops EXIF and GPS data, which also protects your privacy.
