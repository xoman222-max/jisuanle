How to use

Protect copies of IDs or your photos by stamping text such as “For identity verification only”. A tiled diagonal watermark is hardest to remove. Everything runs in your browser — your images are never uploaded.

FAQ

What should I write on ID copies?

State the purpose and recipient, e.g. “For XYZ Bank account opening only”.

Can I use a logo?

Text watermarks only for now.
