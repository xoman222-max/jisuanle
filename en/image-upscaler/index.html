How to use

Quick mode uses multi-step resampling with optional sharpening. AI mode runs Real-ESRGAN locally (first use downloads ~5 MB model plus the engine) and restores detail in small or blurry photos. Everything runs in your browser — your images are never uploaded.

FAQ

Is AI mode free?

Yes, it runs on your device.

Why is AI mode slow?

It processes the image in tiles; a GPU (WebGPU) makes it much faster.
