Privacy Policy

Your data

Calculators and image tools run locally in your browser. We do not collect the numbers you enter or the images you process.

AI features

Only when you press “AI captions” or “Translate”, a downscaled image or your text is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate the result. We do not store it.

Cookies and ads

We may show Google AdSense ads, which can use cookies to personalize ads. You can manage this in Google’s ad settings. We count page views anonymously without personal profiles.
